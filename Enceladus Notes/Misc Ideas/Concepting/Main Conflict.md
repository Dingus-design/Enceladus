 Fear of past trauma
 - Core Struggle: Calypso remembers the past timelines, Titania doesn't
 - World Context: Space life is fading and life is static
 - Personal Friction: Calypso remembers the past -> pushes Titania away. Titania wants to prove this lifetime will be different (with vulnerability and fighting)
 - Theme: Overcoming past grief to connect vs isolation and ultimate death

Safety vs Unknown
- Core Struggle: Routine vs transformation (humans vs nature)
 - World Context: Space traffic is dying; Titania is working a sterile job
 - Personal Friction: Titania fears leaving what she's always known (order/tech); Calypso despises the cybernetic race for not living and having rigid systems
 - Theme: Leaving the known (morally grey system) for authentic connection vs a dying stagnant routine -> escaping matrix

Difference in Nature
- Core Struggle: Cypernetic vs divine existence + nature
 - World Context: Energy in space is running low; tensions between species
 - Personal Friction: Titania's tech detects Calypso as a security threat; Calypso fears being captured at the hands of the ISO
 - Theme: Adapting to each others differences vs physical barriers keeping each other apart

 Duty vs Rebellion
- Core Struggle: Obeying vs defiance of authority
 - World Context:The ISO strictly controls cybernetic humans and views celstial beings as threats
 - Personal Friction: Titania is ordered by the ISO to investigate Calypso; Calypso sees Titania as a slave to the ISO's corrupt system until Titania refuses to follow orders
 - Theme: Betraying career, loyalty, and life path to vs losing her real soulmate
